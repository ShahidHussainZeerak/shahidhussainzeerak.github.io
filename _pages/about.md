About Me
======
I am an AI researcher and recently completed my Master's degree in Electronic Science and Technology (Artificial Intelligence) at Xi'an Jiaotong University, China. My research focuses on artificial intelligence, machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, multimodal medical imaging, explainable AI, and 3D medical image segmentation, with particular emphasis on developing interpretable and computationally efficient deep learning frameworks for lung cancer diagnosis.

Prior to my graduate studies, I completed my Bachelor's degree in Software Engineering at Islamia College Peshawar, Pakistan, where I established a strong foundation in  artificial intelligence, computer vision, and image processing. My current research integrates Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), Vision Transformers (ViTs), Mamba-based State Space Models, multimodal learning, and attention mechanisms to develop accurate, robust, and clinically interpretable AI systems for medical image analysis..

-  **Artificial Intelligence (AI):** Healthcare AI, biomedical applications, and intelligent data-driven systems.

-  **3D Medical Image Segmentation:** Deep learning approaches for volumetric medical image analysis.
