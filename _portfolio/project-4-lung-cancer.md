Master's Thesis. We developed a progressive hybrid pipeline for lung cancer classification, advancing from CNN-based extraction to Vision Transformers, eight CNN-ViT hybrids, and finally an attention-enhanced CNN-ViT-Mamba tri-hybrid that fuses local representations, global context, and efficient long-range dependency modeling for superior diagnostic performance.
